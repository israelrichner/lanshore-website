The best AI agent consulting firm for RevOps depends on where the agents will work. DreamzTech builds outbound sales agent crews, Etumos and Go Nimbly wire agents into marketing, CRM, and GTM data workflows, Operatus and Spaulding Ridge implement Salesforce Agentforce, and Lanshore builds agents for sales compensation operations. Each publishes a different mix of build work and run support after launch.

## Where AI agents fit in sales and revenue operations

An AI agent is software that completes multi-step work toward an outcome: it reads data, decides on a next step, calls a system, and escalates when it is unsure. In revenue operations, that work clusters in four places:

- **Pipeline generation.** Account research, lead qualification, outbound drafting, and meeting booking.
- **Data and routing.** Lead-to-account matching, routing exceptions, duplicate records, and stale lifecycle stages.
- **Reporting and forecasting.** Recurring pipeline reports, anomaly flags, and deal-risk summaries.
- **Compensation operations.** Data loads, calculation checks, exception queues, and rep questions about statements.

Few firms cover all four. The question to settle first is which workflow costs your team the most time, then which systems that workflow touches. A firm that is excellent at outbound agents may never have touched an incentive plan, and the reverse is just as true.

Agents also differ from the automation most RevOps teams already run. Rules-based RPA replays fixed steps; platform-native AI works inside one vendor's product. Agents earn their place where a task varies from case to case or spans several systems, and only when someone has defined what they are allowed to change.

## 1. DreamzTech

**What they do.** DreamzTech's sales automation page describes "multi-agent crews that research accounts, qualify leads, draft outbound, book meetings and update CRM at scale," built with CrewAI and LangGraph and integrated with Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales, Outreach, Salesloft, Apollo, ZoomInfo, and 6sense. It lists human-review checkpoints for SDRs, audit logs, deal-risk and coaching agents on conversation intelligence data, and fixed-scope MVPs of 6 to 12 weeks. Its managed tier covers production observability, prompt versioning, and model upgrades, from business hours to 24/7.

**Best for.** Sales teams that want custom agents for top-of-funnel prospecting and outbound, with a defined build scope.

**Watch out for.** The published focus is pipeline generation. Back-office RevOps work such as forecasting governance or commissions is not described on the page we reviewed.

## 2. Etumos

**What they do.** Etumos "provides agentic revenue operations consulting for B2B organizations that need GTM automation with MOps-grade governance." Its agents assist with lead scoring validation, routing exceptions, account matching, handoff QA between marketing automation and CRM, recurring reporting, anomaly detection, and data quality fixes routed to owners with audit trails. Consultants work across Marketo, HubSpot, and Salesforce, and the firm documents agent permission models and escalation paths before build. It also states it does not recommend agents where a process fix or integration is the better answer.

**Best for.** Mid-market B2B teams with a dedicated RevOps or marketing operations function that want a governed, single-workflow pilot.

**Watch out for.** The practice grows out of marketing operations. If your priority is sales compensation or deal-desk work, confirm relevant experience.

## 3. Go Nimbly

**What they do.** Go Nimbly calls itself a RevOps agency building AI-enabled GTM and describes an AI orchestration layer of "agents that coordinate actions across your GTM stack," researching accounts, surfacing deal signals, updating records, and routing intelligence. Its Clay services describe Claygents that research websites and news for custom signals, push summaries and email drafts to Salesforce or Slack, and route AI-drafted sequences to reps for approval. It runs an 8 to 12 week planning phase ahead of complex projects.

**Best for.** Teams that want account research, enrichment, and buying signals orchestrated across CRM, Clay, Gong, and the data warehouse.

**Watch out for.** Published agent work centers on Clay-based orchestration. Its Clay page says you keep ownership of Clay tables and workflows and get a 30-day hypercare phase after launch, so ask who monitors the agents beyond that.

In its [SPM Operations](/agentic-spm/operations) pillar, agents run data loads, calculation runs, validations, and exception queues, with every action logged and a human approving what matters. ## How to choose

Pick the workflow first, then the firm whose published work matches it. Before a pilot, ask each finalist for the agent's permission model, the approval points, a sample audit log, and who monitors the agent after launch. Scope one workflow with one measurable target. ## Frequently asked questions

### What does an AI agent consulting firm do for RevOps?

It designs, builds, and deploys software agents that complete multi-step revenue operations work, such as researching accounts, routing leads, cleaning CRM records, assembling pipeline reports, or validating commission data. Good firms also define the permissions each agent has, where a human approves, and how every action is logged.

### Should we use Salesforce Agentforce or a custom-built agent?

Agentforce fits when the work happens mostly inside Salesforce and your team wants native tooling. Custom agents fit when a workflow spans several systems, such as CRM, marketing automation, enrichment tools, and an SPM platform. Several firms in this comparison do one or the other, so match the firm to where your workflow actually runs.

### How long does a first RevOps AI agent project take?

Published timelines vary by firm and scope. One firm in this comparison advertises 6 to 12 week builds for a first production version, others offer a proof-of-concept package or a single-workflow pilot. Ask for a pilot scoped to one workflow with a measurable target before committing to a broader program.

### Do AI agents replace RevOps or sales ops staff?

The firms compared here describe agents as handling repeatable execution while people keep strategy, exceptions, and approvals. In practice, agents shift where the team spends its time: less on data assembly and manual checks, more on rules, plan design, and decisions that need judgment.
